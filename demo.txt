The history of science tracks how human understanding of the natural and physical world has evolved from ancient practical observations into modern systematic inquiry.
Ancient Beginnings
• Egypt and Mesopotamia (c. 3000–1200 BCE): Built early math, decimal systems, geometry for farming, calendars, and recorded star movements.
• Ancient Greece (c. 600–300 BCE): Shifted toward rational, non-mythological explanations of nature through thinkers like Thales, Aristotle, and Pythagoras.
• Global Contributions: Ancient China, India, and the Islamic Golden Age advanced medicine, optics, alchemy, and astronomy (such as the compass and Ibn al-Haytham's physics).
